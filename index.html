FAQ

Questions.

Does KeyClack record what I type?

No. It only reacts to key presses to play a sound. Nothing is saved, and the app doesn’t connect to the internet at all.

Why does it need Input Monitoring?

That’s how macOS lets an app notice key presses while you type in other apps. Without it KeyClack can’t play sounds outside its own window.

Which Macs are supported?

Any Mac with macOS 13 Ventura or later — both Apple Silicon and Intel. Built-in and external keyboards both work.

Can I use my own sounds?

Yes. KeyClack supports Mechvibes sound packs — drag a pack folder or .zip into the KeyClack window.

How do I turn it off?

Click the keyboard icon in the menu bar and switch off Key Sounds, or choose Quit.
